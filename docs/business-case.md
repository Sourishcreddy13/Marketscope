# MarketScope Business Case

## Problem
A retail brokerage wants a self-service platform where customers can onboard, discover stocks, maintain watchlists, place simulated trades, and understand portfolio performance without relying on a manual operations workflow.

## Target users
- **Customer**: manages their account, watchlists, orders, and portfolio analytics.
- **ADMIN**: manages users, stock catalog data, order queue, synthetic market prices, and platform statistics.

## Success metrics
- AC-01 through AC-10 are implemented and automatically tested.
- All eight NFRs are continuously enforced.
- The application runs locally with one SQLite database and a simple frontend/backend startup flow.
- Every feature change has a specification, generated tests, evaluator evidence, and PR review history.

## Value proposition
MarketScope demonstrates an end-to-end brokerage workflow while keeping market execution synthetic. It emphasizes deterministic domain behavior, auditable state transitions, secure role boundaries, fixed-point financial calculations, and AI-native delivery discipline.
